Vollständige Beschreibung

Shape how Axpo grows. As Program Lead IT Integration, you'll own the end-to-end integration of new subsidiaries into our Group IT backbone – leading complex programs with real impact, from day one to stable operations.

What you will do

  • Own and lead end-to-end IT integration programs for subsidiaries such as Axpo Bulgaria, Volkswind, Urbasolar and Axpo Poland, and support planning for future acquisitions
  • Set up each integration as a well-governed program: scope, stream organization, integrated plan, budget, and risk and issue management
  • Prepare and run cut-overs with rehearsed runbooks, define go/no-go criteria and safeguard business continuity throughout
  • Lead a dedicated, cross-functional team of around 15 experts in an agile, pod-based way of working
  • Manage stakeholders from Group Senior Management to Division and Subsidiary Management, and report progress, risks and decisions transparently
  • Continuously improve Axpo's IT integration playbook based on experience and lessons learned from each wave

What you bring & who you are

  • Experience leading large, complex IT programs in an international environment, ideally post-merger integrations, carve-ins or large-scale migrations
  • Broad technical understanding of IT infrastructure, identity and access management, Microsoft 365 and modern workplace technologies
  • Strong stakeholder management skills, comfortable engaging people from senior management to local teams
  • Able to lead through influence and functional lines rather than formal authority
  • Ideally, experience with agile, pod-based ways of working (e.g., Scrum, Kanban, SAFe)
  • Fluent in English
  • German and/or French are a strong advantage

Even if you don't meet all the requirements, we look forward to your application.

About the team

You'll lead a dedicated, cross-functional team of around 15 experts from Identity Management, Workplace, Collaboration, Network, Security and more – working side by side with colleagues from the subsidiary as equal members of one team.
